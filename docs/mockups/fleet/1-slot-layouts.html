Three ways to lay out your 20 slots. Each shows the same airline: 23.4 h flown, so 4 slots open (2 to start, +1 at 10 h and 20 h). Two are filled, two are open and empty, slot 5 opens at 30 h. Click a filled slot to open that airplane; click an empty one to add an aircraft. Click an option to pick it.
