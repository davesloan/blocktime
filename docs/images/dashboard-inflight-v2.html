Step through the flight with Next; Back steps back. Click the pill to collapse the header. ATC's words and times are from the KSAN → KSJC leg of 2026-11-25; the gauges and the lamps left wrong (steps 2, 5 and 10) are made up.
  • What changed after v1:
    • the altimeter has three hands (D-202);
    • no frequencies (D-191);
    • the ATIS letter as its ICAO word (JULIETT);
    • the arrival ATIS readouts from the descent, and DEST ATIS in cruise from 200 nm out;
    • the plan's STAR and landing runway, flagged when ATC's differ;
    • none of milestone 15's predictions (top of climb, top of descent, landing time, fuel against plan, Early / Delayed).
  • Laid out for Live at the smallest window (1100 × 760, so 900 px beside the sidebar). With the header collapsed the tallest view, the arrival ATIS, fits.
    • The ATC card runs across the panel and the readouts sit under it, four a row. Side by side, the slots cut off "DESCEND VIA SILCN6".
    • Each card's aside (a qualifier, a source, or the time of the call) is on its title row, so the amber window holds only the value. Pre-Flight's readouts would change to match.
    • LATEST is the slot's placard lit amber, like an annunciator, instead of a separate tag.
    • ALTITUDE and ROUTE keep their places from taxi to the air; on the ground TAXI is on the right.
    • Wind reads as on Pre-Flight: "Variable / 4 kt", "360° / 4 kt".
  • The altimeter reads like a clock:
    • the short, wide hand is the ten thousands;
    • the long hand is the thousands;
    • the skinny hand is the hundreds, one turn per 1,000 ft.
    At FL400 the short hand is on 4 and the other two are on 0. The window under it is the setting: STD, or 30.09 / 30.19.
  • For an emergency, in every phase: SOULS (passengers plus the crew BlockTime pays for: 2 pilots and the BBJ2's 3 flight attendants) and FUEL REMAINING in hours and minutes at the plan's average burn (SimBrief's endurance for its takeoff fuel, about 4,820 lb/h on this leg). It counts down with the fuel and never jumps with thrust; with no SimBrief plan it shows a dash. The fuel gauge's window has the pounds.
  • Radio altitude: below 2,000 ft above the ground the altimeter flips to a 0–3,000 ft radio altitude dial (the bottom 500 ft takes half the dial, for the flare), and flips back above 3,000 ft, so terrain under the approach can't make it flicker. A go-around flips it back at once, and it returns on the next short final. On the ground it shows the radio face. The short-final HEIGHT readout is gone: the dial shows it.
  • A flashing lamp is a switch the debrief would mark down right now. Lit means on, dark means off; flashing means fix it (1 s lit, 1 s dark).
  • Flips: a card flips when its value changes, and several flip in a wave from left to right (60 ms apart). The speed gauge flips when it switches between ground speed and airspeed. Watch step 8 (one call, three slots) and every phase change.
  • The ATC card keeps one slot per kind of instruction; each changes only when ATC changes it. LATEST marks what the last call changed. Without a qualifier the aside is the time of the call.
